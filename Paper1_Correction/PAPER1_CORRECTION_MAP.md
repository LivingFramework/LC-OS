## Guardrail for the A1–A10 collision

The published paper uses `A1–A10` for two incompatible sets of controls. Batch 1 must add one concise historical-disclosure note at the first explanatory occurrence:

> “The published paper uses the A1–A10 labels inconsistently in later sections. This correction preserves the historical record but does not make control-specific outcome attributions.”

No new naming system is authorised. The correction must not invent or introduce a replacement taxonomy.
